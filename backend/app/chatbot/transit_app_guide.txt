# Smart Transit Companion - User Guide

## What is Smart Transit Companion (An App) ?

Smart Transit Companion is an AI-powered mobile application designed to revolutionize public transportation in Sri Lanka. It's your personal travel assistant that helps you navigate buses, trains, tuk-tuks, and other transport modes efficiently and seamlessly.

## Key Features 

Multi-Modal Journey Planning,Real-Time Updates,Personalized Travel Recommendations,Multi-Language Support ,Fare Optimization are the main features of the app.

### Multi-Modal Journey Planning
The app plans complete journeys using multiple transport modes. Whether you need to take a bus, then a train, and finally a tuk-tuk to reach your destination, our AI agents will create the optimal route for you.

### Real-Time Updates
Get live information about bus and train schedules, delays, cancellations, and route changes. Never wait at a bus stop wondering if your bus is coming.

### Personalized Travel Recommendations
The app learns your preferences for travel modes, timings, budget constraints, and accessibility needs to provide customized route suggestions.

### Multi-Language Support
Available in Sinhala, Tamil, and English with voice assistance capabilities for easy interaction in your preferred language.

### Fare Optimization
Find the cheapest travel combinations, including information about passes, discounts, and special offers to save money on your journeys.

## How Our AI Agents Work

### Data Aggregation Agent
Continuously collects real-time transport data from official APIs, operator websites, and crowdsourced information to keep you informed.

### Route Optimization Agent
Calculates the fastest, cheapest, or most convenient routes based on your preferences, considering travel time, wait time, and costs.

### Disruption Management Agent
Monitors transport services for delays, strikes, or route changes and automatically suggests alternative routes when disruptions occur.

### Personalization Agent
Learns from your travel patterns and preferences to provide increasingly accurate and personalized recommendations.

### Language & Accessibility Agent
Ensures the app is accessible to everyone, including differently-abled users, with voice commands, screen reader support, and visual adaptations.

### Local Knowledge Agent
Incorporates community-contributed information, especially valuable for rural and suburban areas where official data might be limited.

## Getting Started

### First-Time Setup
1. Download the Smart Transit Companion app
2. Select your preferred language (Sinhala, Tamil, or English)
3. Set up your profile with accessibility preferences if needed
4. Grant location permissions for accurate route planning
5. Add your frequently visited places (home, work, etc.)

### Planning Your First Journey
1. Open the app and tap "Plan Journey"
2. Enter your starting point and destination
3. Choose your travel preferences (fastest, cheapest, most comfortable)
4. Select preferred transport modes or let AI choose for you
5. Review the suggested routes and select your preferred option
6. Start your journey with step-by-step guidance
